About Us

Support and Help Caregiver Institute

The Support and Help Caregiver Institute is a registered non-governmental organisation (NGO) in Nigeria, dedicated to the people at the heart of care — the caregivers. We believe that caregiving is a heroic act, and we exist to transform the landscape of care by equipping those heroes with the skills, strength, and support they need.

  • Certified Professional Caregiver Course
  • Specialized Care Modules (Dementia & Alzheimer's, Palliative & Hospice, Maternal & Child Health, Disability & Special Needs)
  • The "Hero's Resilience" Support Group for emotional & mental health
  • Economic Empowerment Skills for sustainable caregiver livelihoods

We provide professional training, emotional support, and practical economic tools that turn passion into professional competence, ensuring that every recipient of care receives dignity, quality, and compassion.

Why We Exist

Closing the Caregiving Gap in Nigeria

In Nigeria, the demand for trained, compassionate caregivers far outstrips the supply. Unpaid family caregivers often face burnout, isolation, and financial hardship, while professional caregivers may lack standardized, high-quality training.

  • Preventing Burnout: Providing mental health, resilience, and respite support.
  • Building Community: Creating a strong network for shared knowledge and peer support.
  • Advocating for Dignity: Promoting better working conditions and respect for caregivers.
